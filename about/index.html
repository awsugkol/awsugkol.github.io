Our Mission & Story

The AWS User Group Kolkata (AWSUGKOL) is an official, community-led group based in Kolkata, India. We are united by a shared passion for Amazon Web Services, cloud architecture, and modern software engineering.

Our focus spans across Cloud Infrastructure, Serverless Computing, DevOps & Automation, AI/ML (Amazon Bedrock), and Enterprise Scalability. Led by dedicated volunteers, we bridge the gap between academic concepts and real-world production deployment.
